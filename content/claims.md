# Claims & Protection

This is the page that saves you the most grief. There are **two separate claim
systems** and they protect different things.

| System | Protects | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| **FTB Chunks** | land — chunks in the world | your chunk allowance |
| **AeroClaims** | ships — the blocks on a sub-level | spends FTB Chunks claims |

You need both. Claiming land does **not** protect a ship, and claiming a ship
does **not** protect the ground under it.

---

## Land claims (FTB Chunks)

Open the map with **`M`** and click chunks to claim them.

- You get **500 chunks** to spend
- Claims are **full-height columns** — from bedrock to sky. There is no such
  thing as claiming "just the surface"

> [!important] Claims go all the way up
> A ship flying over someone else's claimed chunk is *inside that claim*, at any
> altitude. Owning the ship doesn't help. If you can't interact with your own
> contraption somewhere, check whose chunk you're above.

### Teams

You can form a party so friends can build with you.

**Everyone brings their own allowance.** Joining a team doesn't mean sharing one
person's 500 chunks — each member's allowance adds to the team pool, and
everything the team claims is usable by everyone in it.

---

## Ship claims (AeroClaims)

Ships are protected separately, with a **claim block** placed on the ship.

Each claim covers **250 ship blocks**. Bigger ship, more claims — and each one
spends a chunk from your FTB Chunks allowance.

> [!warning] The rule that costs people their ships
> **Place → claim → name.** In that order.
>
> A claim is bound to the ship's internal ID. Picking a ship up with a portable
> structure item and putting it back down creates a **brand new ID**, which
> orphans the claim — the ship is now unprotected and the old claim is wasted.
>
> **Don't capture a ship you've already claimed.**

### Naming your ship

```
/shipname <name>
```

Stand on the ship and run it. The name shows on the claim screen. `/shipname info`
tells you the current name, `/shipname clear` removes it.

Only the ship's owner can rename a claimed ship.

---

## If you can't break or place a block

Work through these in order:

1. **Are you inside someone else's claim?** Open the map with `M` — claims are
   full-height, so check even if you're flying high above them
2. **Is the ship claimed by someone else?** Ship claims are separate from land
3. **Did you re-place the ship after claiming it?** Then the claim is orphaned —
   see the warning above. You'll need to claim it again

Still stuck? Ask in **[Discord](https://discord.gg/AnFUh5vTz6)** with your
coordinates and what you were trying to do.
