# Aero Radio

Radios play live stations that everyone hears in sync. Tune in and you join the song where
it is right now, the same as a real radio. Place a radio block, or carry a portable radio
in your hand.

## Stations

| Frequency | Station | What it plays |
|---|---|---|
| **88.8** | Lofi Lounge | Lofi beats, all day |
| **90.1** | Christmas FM | Carols and holiday music |
| **92.5** | Skyline Mall | Jazz, bossa and lounge: shopping-mall music |
| **95.5** | Chill Pop | Chill electronic and synth pop |
| **100.0** | Aero FM | Player song requests |

The first time you hear a song it downloads in a few seconds. After that it plays from
your cache.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/radio library [search]` | List the songs on the server |
| `/radio request <link>` | Ask for a song on Aero FM. Staff review it first |
| `/radio myrequests` | See what happened to your requests |
| `/radio mystation create <name>` | Make your own station from songs in the library |
| `/radio mystation add <song>` / `remove <song>` | Edit your station's playlist |
| `/radio mystation freq <freq>` | Pick its frequency |
| `/radio mystation shuffle on/off`, `loop on/off` | Play order |
| `/radio mystation delete` | Remove your station |

## Music credits

Every song on the radio is free to share: CC0, Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), or
public domain. The artists include HoliznaCC0, Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), the US Air
Force and Navy bands, and many Free Music Archive artists. Songs were converted to mono
and loudness-normalised for the radio. **[Full credits: every track, artist, licence and link →](radio-credits)**

Requests have to follow the same rule, so commercial songs can't be added.
