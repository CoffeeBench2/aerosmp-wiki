# Economy & Rewards

The currency is **spurs**, and it comes from Create: Numismatics — so money is a
physical item you can put on a belt, not a number in a menu.

You start with **300 spurs** the first time you `/spawn` into the world. That is
a one-time payment and it is enough to get going, not enough to skip anything.

---

## Spurs

Spurs are coins. They stack, they drop when you die, and they can be stored,
transported and automated like any other item. Numismatics adds the whole chain
around them — vaults for storage, and machines that let you build actual shops.

Because coins are items, **the usual rule applies: don't carry your savings into
a fight.** Nobody is going to refund a purse dropped in lava.

## Daily reward

```
/daily
```

Claimable every **20 hours**. It pays out **items and XP only** — it never
touches the economy, so nobody can inflate the currency by logging in.

There's a streak. Leave it longer than **double** the interval and the streak
resets, so claiming at roughly the same time each day is what keeps it alive.
A missed day isn't a disaster; a missed week is.

## Voting

Voting for the server on a listing site pays you, and it's the one reward that
scales with sticking around.

| | First vote | Grows to |
|---|---|---|
| Spurs | 2 | **10** |
| Diamonds | 1 | **5** |

The reward ramps up as you keep voting and then holds at the cap. **The first 30
votes are rewarded** — after that, voting still helps the server, it just stops
paying you.

Two things worth knowing:

- **There is no streak.** Miss a day and you lose nothing. The count only ever
  goes up
- **Rewards are banked while you're offline.** Vote from your phone at work; the
  payout is waiting when you next log in

## Where the rest of your money comes from

- **Quest rewards** — see [[quests]]. Chapters pay in their own mod's items,
  which is often worth more than coins
- **Selling to other players.** Numismatics exists to make player shops work.
  The Discord is where most trade gets arranged
- **Automation.** This is a Create server. The intended answer to "how do I get
  more of something" is usually a machine
