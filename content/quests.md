# Quests

A 200-mod sandbox with no direction is how people bounce off modded Minecraft.
The quest book is the answer to "what do I even do now" — **43 chapters**, from
chopping your first log to flying a ship you designed.

You are never forced into it. Ignore it entirely and nothing is withheld from
you. But it is also the fastest way to learn what's actually in this pack,
because most of the chapters exist to introduce a mod you didn't know was
installed.

---

## Opening the book

The quest book is an item. You get one automatically on your first join — check
your inventory. Lost it? Craft another from a book and any dye, or ask in
**[Discord](https://discord.gg/AnFUh5vTz6)**.

## How it's laid out

Chapters come in two kinds, and they behave differently.

**The progression chapters** are the spine. They run in order — getting
established, learning Create, the first machines, and eventually airships. If
you're new, follow these and let the rest wait.

**The addon chapters** are one per Create addon in the pack. There are far more
of these, and they are deliberately *not* a sequence. Each one is a self-guided
tour of a single mod: what it adds, what it's for, and what you can build with
it. Dip into whichever one catches your eye.

> [!tip] The addon chapters are the pack's index
> This pack ships a lot of Create addons and nobody memorises them. If you ever
> think "I wonder if this pack has a mod for X", the chapter list is the
> fastest way to find out.

## Rewards

Rewards are drawn from **the chapter's own mod**. A chapter about a given addon
pays out in that addon's items — so finishing it leaves you holding the things
you just learned to use, rather than a pile of generic loot.

Difficulty is tiered, and the later tiers are genuinely demanding. A tier-3 gate
can ask for 32 Precision Mechanisms, 16 Sturdy Sheets and 8 Refined Radiance.
That is not a typo, and it is not meant to be done on your first evening.

**Claim Blocks appear as rewards too**, which means land comes from playing
rather than from asking staff for more. See [[claims]].

## If a quest looks impossible

Some gates are meant to be long-term goals rather than next steps. Before
assuming something is broken:

1. **Check the tier.** Later chapters assume an established base and automation
2. **Check you have the mod's earlier chapter done** — addon chapters often
   assume you've met the mod's basics
3. **Ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/AnFUh5vTz6)** — if a quest genuinely
   can't be completed we want to know, and that has happened before

> [!warning] Reward says "Missing Item"?
> That's a bug on our side, not something you did wrong. Report it in Discord
> with the chapter name and we'll fix the quest — you won't lose the reward.
